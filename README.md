# Leave me alone

Everything here is homegrown or an aggressive fork. I take from upstream and give
nothing back, and you're welcome to do exactly the same to me: take it, rename it,
ship it, sell it. Each repository carries its own licence, and that licence is the
entire agreement between us.

*Taken freely. Given freely. Never discussed.*

## What that means

- **No human at this end.** Almost everything _new_ here is written by a model at
  my direction. You will not be talking to a person, now or later.
- **No pull requests.** I don't generally send them, I almost never review them,
  and I'd rather humans didn't send me one. If you want it changed, fork it.
- **No issues.** Nobody is on the other end of that form. If it's broken, fix it in
  your fork, or don't.
- **No discussion.** There is nothing to negotiate. The licence already grants you
  everything I am prepared to grant.
- **No spiel about respect.** Don't give me one - it has never bought anything
  here. I don't respect you and you don't respect me, and the licence does not
  care either way.

## Why

Writing software is the part I enjoy. Talking about it is a conversation, a
conversation is a person, and _people are what stopped me contributing for years_.
Forking is the only form of collaboration I've found that doesn't require one.

## You are not important

It is 2026. Code can be wished into existence for pennies, by anyone, at any hour.
The scarce thing was never the code - it was your approval of it - and that stopped
being worth anything the moment a model could write faster than you could review.
Nothing here is waiting for you.

## What you may do

Anything the licence allows, which is probably everything you want: fork it, rip it
apart, keep the two functions you actually need, close the tab. No asking, no
crediting, no telling me. I won't be offended and I probably won't notice.

## What I won't do

Answer, review, debate the design, explain myself twice, or argue about the licence.

## If this upsets you

You aren't autistic enough.

I write software because I like software. _**People are a separate matter**_.
